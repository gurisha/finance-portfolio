# Finance & Analytics Portfolio

This repository contains a selection of finance and analytics projects demonstrating my hands-on experience across FP&A, financial reporting, risk analysis, and business profitability.

These projects represent a snapshot of my broader analytical skill set and are designed to reflect how I approach real-world financial problems - from data analysis and modeling to executive-level interpretation and communication.

Each project simulates practical workflows commonly used in corporate finance and analytics teams.

## What Each Project Includes

Each project folder contains:

* An Excel model with cleaned data, calculations, dashboards, or analytical frameworks  
* A PDF executive summary outlining key insights and business implications  
* A short video walkthrough presenting findings as if reporting to senior leadership (CFO-style presentation)

The focus is not only on technical analysis, but also on translating numbers into clear, business-relevant insights.

## Project Areas Covered

* FP&A forecasting and performance analysis  
* Financial reporting and variance interpretation  
* Credit risk modeling and risk assessment  
* Revenue and profitability analysis  
* Operational and business data analytics
