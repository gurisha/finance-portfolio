# Netflix Financial Reporting & Analysis

# Objective

To simulate a full month-end close process for a streaming-based subscription business by transforming raw general ledger activity into reconciled financial statements and executive-level insights.

# Data Used

The analysis was built from transaction-level general ledger data, including journal entries with balanced debit and credit postings, departmental classification, and account categorization. The dataset reflects realistic revenue recognition, expense accruals, and cash activity for a single reporting period.

# Analysis Performed

* Constructed and validated a reconciled Trial Balance
* Prepared Income Statement and Balance Sheet from GL data
* Ensured statement linkage through retained earnings flow
* Performed profitability and liquidity ratio analysis
* Developed management-level commentary summarizing performance drivers and financial position

# Key Findings

* Subscription revenue totaled $4.2M for the period
* Operating expenses of $2.49M resulted in operating profitability of $1.71M (presented using accounting sign convention)
* Content licensing represented the primary cost driver
* Liquidity remained stable, with current assets exceeding current liabilities
* Gross margin of around 74% reflects the scalability of the subscription model

# Business Implications

The results indicate a scalable revenue structure supported by disciplined cost management and stable short-term liquidity. Continued monitoring of content costs and marketing efficiency will be critical to sustaining margin expansion as the business grows. The financial position provides flexibility to reinvest in content and platform development without near-term liquidity pressure.
