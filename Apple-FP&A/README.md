# Apple FP&A Forecasting & Performance Analysis

## Objective

To simulate an FP&A performance review of Apple’s recent financial results and develop a structured, scenario-based revenue forecast for FY2026 to support forward planning and investment decision-making.

## Data Used

Publicly available financial data from Apple Inc. Form 10-K filings for FY2023–FY2025, including revenue by segment, operating income, expenses, and net income. All historical data was consolidated and cleaned within Excel prior to analysis.

## Analysis Performed

* Revenue trend analysis (FY2023–FY2025)
* Product vs. Services revenue mix evaluation
* Operating margin and profitability assessment
* Operating leverage review
* Scenario-based FY2026 revenue forecast (Base / Upside / Downside)
* Executive dashboard construction for performance monitoring

## Key Findings

* Revenue growth accelerated in FY2025, building on an already stabilized base.
* Services revenue continues to outpace product growth, improving revenue quality and predictability.
* Operating margin expanded consistently, indicating disciplined cost management alongside growth.
* Scenario analysis suggests FY2026 revenue remains resilient even under downside assumptions, with services providing structural support.

## Business Implication

The business enters FY2026 from a position of strength, supported by an improving revenue mix and sustained margin discipline. Scenario framing enables balanced planning—maintaining investment discipline under the base case while remaining positioned to capture incremental upside if services momentum continues.
